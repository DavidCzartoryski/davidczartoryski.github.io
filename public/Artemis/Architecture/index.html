What this page is, and is not

What it is: a conceptual logic map of how Artemis is meant to move from signals to decisions. The failure, signal, trigger, effect and response definitions are a reviewed public summary. Every selected algorithm is Pending, and so is the routing of individual signals to data families.

What it is not: a claim that every real-world metric or failure is covered, or that any model or detector is trained, deployed or hardware-qualified. A definition here is not evidence that the data is collected or that the behavior was tested on hardware.

Model and actions: the pyramid describes the intended architecture. This page does not run an LLM, infer causes, connect to a fleet or execute fixes.

Public sources: vendor metric names, commands and failure behavior come from public vendor documentation and published papers, linked on the failure paths and responses that use them. Those vendors and authors did not review this map.
